**Does the app connect to the internet?**
Yes. It sends your typed input to the AI model and receives an answer back. 

**Is my data stored elsewhere?**
LobeChat keeps your files on your computer. It does not upload your history to external servers unless you choose to share it.

**Can I use multiple models?**
Yes. You can add keys for various providers in the settings section. Use the dropdown menu above the chat box to switch between them.

**What happens if the screen freezes?**
